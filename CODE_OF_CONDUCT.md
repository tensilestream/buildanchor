# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the BuildAnchor project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be welcoming, inclusive, and respectful
- communicate clearly and assume good faith
- focus on what is best for the project and its users
- accept constructive feedback
- protect private and security-sensitive information

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized language or imagery, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report non-sensitive unacceptable behavior through GitHub Discussions or an issue using only
the minimum necessary detail. For safety-sensitive reports, use GitHub's private reporting
channel described in `SECURITY.md`; do not post private information publicly. Reports will be
reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially where possible.
